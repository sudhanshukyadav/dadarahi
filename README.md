Distinguished Hindi literary scholar, educator, and poet with over eight decades of contributions to Indian literature and grassroots education. Renowned in the literary community by the pen name "Rahi," I have focused on advancing the modern Hindi Navgeet tradition, drawing inspiration from Mahakavi Suryakant Tripathi 'Nirala' to give voice to social realities, resilience, and marginalized communities. Alongside an extensive career in public education and broadcasting, I have remained deeply committed to youth empowerment, vernacular preservation, and establishing sustainable educational infrastructure for underprivileged students.
